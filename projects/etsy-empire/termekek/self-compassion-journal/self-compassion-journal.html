Printable journal · 30 days · Undated

Be Kind
to Yourself

A gentle 30-day self-compassion journal: notice how you feel, talk to yourself like a good friend would, and end each day with one small kindness for you.
21 pages · US Letter + A4
Start here

How to use this journal

1
Five minutes a day

Pick a time that already exists: with your morning coffee or before you sleep. One page holds two days.

2
Notice, don't judge

Write down how you feel and what happened. There are no wrong answers and no grades.

3
Talk like a friend

Ask: "What would I say to a good friend in this situation?" Then write that to yourself.

4
One small kindness

End the day with one small thing for you: a walk, a glass of water, going to bed on time.

Please note

This journal is a self-care tool, not therapy or medical advice. If you feel unsafe or overwhelmed, please contact a doctor or a local crisis line (US: call or text 988 · UK: Samaritans 116 123 · EU: 112).
