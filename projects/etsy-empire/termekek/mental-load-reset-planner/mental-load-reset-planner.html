How to use your planner

The mental load is the noticing, remembering and planning that keeps a family running. It is real work, even when nobody sees it. This planner makes it visible, so it can be shared.

1
Write it all down

Use the task inventory to list everything that lives in your head. Add what's missing for your family.

2
Mark who does what

For each task: who notices, who plans, who does it. Most couples are surprised by the "notices" column.

3
Talk once, calmly

Use the conversation page to prepare. Pick a quiet time, not the middle of a busy evening.

4
Hand over whole tasks

Not "help me with", but "this is yours, from noticing to finishing". Then try the 7-day reset.
