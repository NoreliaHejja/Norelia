Add-Your-Face
Christmas Friends

40 faceless Christmas characters: add a photo of your child's face and turn them into an elf, Santa, a reindeer, a gingerbread friend and more.
Start here

Two ways to add a face

1
On paper (no tech needed)

Print a character page. Print or cut out a photo of your child's face, slightly bigger than the white circle, and glue it on top. Great for classrooms, bulletin boards and cards.

2
Digitally (phone or computer)

Open a PNG from the download folder in a free app such as Canva, Google Slides or PowerPoint. Place your photo on the layer behind the character and move it until the face shows through the hole. Save and share.

3
Make a bulletin board

Print the "OUR LITTLE ELVES" letters and line up the whole class or family underneath.

What's included

40 PNG characters with transparent background and a see-through face hole (high resolution), 40 printable pages, bulletin board letters, US Letter + A4.
