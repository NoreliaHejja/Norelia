How to use your planner

Screens are not the enemy. Long, empty days are. This planner gives the break a shape so that screens become one part of the day, not the whole day.

1
Fill in the agreement together

Before the first day off, sit down with the kids and agree on a few simple rules. Kids follow rules they helped make.

2
Plan your rhythm

Use the 14-day planner: one outdoor time, one quiet time and one "together" activity per day. Leave space for nothing at all.

3
Fill the boredom jar

Cut out the 32 slips and add your own. "I'm bored" now has an answer that is not a screen.

4
Use the countdown cards

Show the 10-5-1 cards before screens go off and have a landing activity ready. Fewer meltdowns, promise.

First days are bumpy

Many families notice the first two or three days are the hardest. Keep going: it usually gets easier.

Be kind to yourself

A movie on a hard afternoon is not a failure. This is a plan, not a test.
