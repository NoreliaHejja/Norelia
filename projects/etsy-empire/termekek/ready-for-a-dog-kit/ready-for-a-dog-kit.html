How to use this kit

Your child asks for a dog every day. This kit turns that wish into a calm family project: four short weeks, one page at a time. By the end you will know if now is the right time, and your child will have practised real care.

1
Week 1 — Talk & check

Go through the Family Readiness Checklist together after dinner. Be honest; a "not yet" is a good answer too.

2
Week 1 — Do the money

Fill in the Monthly Dog Budget with real local prices. Most families are surprised by the "hidden" lines.

3
Weeks 1–4 — Little Helper Chart

Your child practises picture-based jobs with the cat (or a plush dog). One sticker or colored dot per job.

4
Weeks 1–4 — 30-Day Practice Challenge

A tiny daily task to build the habit. Missed days are fine; just continue the next day.

5
When you decide yes — Cat & Dog Plan

A week-by-week introduction plan that keeps your cat safe and relaxed.

6
Finish — Our Dog Promise

Everyone signs, your child draws the dog they dream of. Pin it to the fridge.

Printing tips

US Letter or A4, "fit to page". Print the chart and challenge pages twice if you have two children. Card stock makes the promise page last longer.

A note for parents

This is a planning aid, not veterinary or legal advice. Ask your vet and local shelter about breeds, costs and rules where you live.

When you decide yes

Cat & Dog Introduction Plan

Go slowly. Move to the next step only when both pets are relaxed. Many introductions take two to four weeks.

BeforePrep

Set up a dog-free room for the cat (food, water, litter, bed). Add shelves or a cat tree. Fit a baby gate. Ask the shelter if the dog has lived with cats.

Days 1–3Smell

Keep them apart. Swap a blanket between rooms so they learn each other's scent. Feed both near the closed door.

Days 4–7See

Short look-only sessions through the baby gate, dog on a leash, treats for calm behaviour. Stop before anyone gets stressed.

Week 2Share

Leashed dog in the same room, cat free to leave and climb high. Five minutes, twice a day. Lots of praise.

Week 3+Live

Supervised time together. The cat's room stays dog-free for good. Never leave them alone together until you are sure.
