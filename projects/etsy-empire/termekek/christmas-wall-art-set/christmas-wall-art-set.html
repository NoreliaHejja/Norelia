Before you print

How to print your wall art

1
Choose your size

Each print fits 8×10 in, A4, 11×14 in and 5×7 in frames: print at 100% for Letter/A4, or let your print shop scale it (the design keeps its margins).

2
Choose your paper

Matte cardstock or photo paper (200–300 g/m²) looks best. For a gallery look, ask a print shop for fine art paper.

3
Frame and style

Group all three on a shelf, hang them in a row above the sofa, or tuck one into the tree branches with a clip.

What's inside

3 prints: "Merry & Bright" (cream and red), "Let It Snow" (forest green and gold), "Joy to the World" (cream and green). Personal use only.
