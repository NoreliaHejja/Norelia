How to use your idea cards

No more 11 p.m. panic scrolling. Pick your ideas in advance, write them on the December planner, and keep the cards next to your bed. Every setup takes about five minutes and uses things you already have.

1
Plan in one sitting

Choose 24 ideas and write the numbers on the December planner. Save the easiest ones for busy school nights.

2
Cut out the props

The elf-size signs make any setup look finished. Print them on card stock and fold them into little tents.

3
Set a reminder

A 9 p.m. phone alarm is the best elf helper ever invented.

4
Keep it kind

Pair a setup with a small kindness mission. The kids will remember how it felt, not how fancy it was.

Safety first

Keep scissors, small candies and string out of reach of little children. Adult setup only.

Good to know

"Christmas Elf" is a generic name for this activity. Not affiliated with any elf doll brand: use any toy you have.

Ideas 1–6

1Marshmallow Bath

The elf relaxes in a mug or bowl full of mini marshmallows, with a tiny towel on its head.

You need: Mug, mini marshmallows, napkin
2Toilet Paper Snowman

Stack three rolls, add a scarf and a paper face. The elf proudly stands next to its snowman.

You need: 3 TP rolls, scarf, paper
3Zip-Line Ride

Tie a string between two chairs and hang the elf from it with a clothespin.

You need: String, clothespin
4Fishing for Goldfish

The elf sits on the sink edge with a pencil fishing rod over a bowl of fish crackers.

You need: Pencil, string, crackers
5Reading Corner

The elf reads a picture book to a circle of stuffed animals.

You need: Book, stuffed toys
6Hide and Seek Note

The elf hides with a note: 'Count to 20 and come find me!'

You need: Note from the props page

Elf emergency list

Forgot again? These three take under one minute.

1. The fridge visit

Pop the elf inside the fridge door. Done.

2. Count to 20

Hide the elf anywhere and leave the "Count to 20" sign on the table.

3. The kindness card

Leave the elf where it was with a "Kindness Mission" sign. Kids love a mission more than a move.
