How to use your planner

Spending the holidays alone can feel heavy, or it can feel free, and often it is both. This planner gives the season a shape, so the hardest days don't arrive unprepared.

1
Decide what you want

Start with "My December, my way". You choose what stays, what goes and what is new this year.

2
One small comfort a day

The 24-day comfort calendar gives each December day one small, kind thing to do for yourself. Tick it off or swap it.

3
Plan Christmas Day

An empty day feels longest. Give it a few anchors: a breakfast, a walk, a call, a film, a meal.

4
Prepare for the hard moments

Fill in your plan and your people list on a good day, so it's ready on a hard one.

This is a plan, not a test

If a day doesn't go as planned, that's fine. Tomorrow is a new page.

You are not the only one

Many people spend the holidays alone, by choice or not. Nothing is wrong with you.

If it gets too heavy
