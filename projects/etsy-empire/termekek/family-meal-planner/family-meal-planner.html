Your 15-minute Sunday plan

Print the weekly plan and grocery list as many times as you like. Sit down for 15 minutes on Sunday, follow the prep list, and the weeknights take care of themselves.

1
Look at the week

Mark sports, late meetings and busy evenings. Those nights get a 20-minute idea from the list.

2
Let your child pick

Use the Kids' Menu Picker: one veggie, one fruit, one favorite. Kids eat more of what they chose.

3
Fill the weekly plan

Breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks. Repeat favorites – nobody needs seven new recipes a week.

4
Write one grocery list

Sorted by aisle, so the shop takes half the time.

5
Prep once

Follow the Sunday Prep checklist. One hour on Sunday saves a lot of weeknight stress.

Printing tips

US Letter and A4 PDFs included. Slip the weekly plan into a sheet protector and use a dry-erase marker to reuse it.

Good to know

A planning tool for busy families, not nutrition or medical advice. Adjust portions and foods to your family's needs and allergies.
