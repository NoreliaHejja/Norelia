How to use your kit

A little Christmas Eve magic that takes ten minutes to set up and gets talked about all year.

1
Print on card stock

Certificates look best on thick white or cream paper. Write the names with a gold or red pen.

2
Fill in the good-things list together

A few days before Christmas, sit down with your child and tick the kind things they did this year. Add their own ideas on the blank lines.

3
Deliver the letter

Fill in the blanks of Santa's letter, fold it into the envelope with the special delivery label and leave it by the tree or the stockings.

4
Feed the reindeer

Mix oats with a pinch of colored sugar, fill small bags and tie on a reindeer food tag. Sprinkle outside on Christmas Eve.

Two certificate styles

A red and gold version and a green version – one per child, or keep one for next year.

Reindeer food tip

Use oats and colored sugar only – no glitter, so it is safe for birds and animals outside.

Christmas Eve

Magic reindeer food recipe

You need

1 cup rolled oats · 2 tablespoons colored sugar (red or green) · small bags or jars · the tags from the previous page
1
Mix

Stir the oats and sugar together in a big bowl. Let your child do the stirring and the "magic words".

2
Fill and tag

Spoon the mix into small bags, tie with ribbon and add a tag. Great for siblings, cousins and classmates too.

3
Sprinkle

Right before bed on Christmas Eve, sprinkle a handful on the lawn or balcony. The moonlight makes it sparkle for the reindeer.

Please note

No glitter, please – it is not safe for birds and animals. Oats and sugar only.
