Before you start

How to play

Find each word from the list in the grid. Words run left to right, top to bottom or diagonally down. They never run backwards. Circle each word you find and tick it off the list.

Tips

Start with the longest words. Look for unusual letters like K, Y or Z. Take a break and come back: fresh eyes find words faster.

Print tip

Print one puzzle at a time, or print the whole book and keep it in a folder. A clipboard makes it easy to solve in an armchair.
