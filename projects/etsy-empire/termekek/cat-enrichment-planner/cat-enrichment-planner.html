Why indoor cats need a plan

Indoor cats are safe, but they still have a hunter's brain. Two short play sessions a day, one puzzle and one cozy high place make a calmer, happier cat – and a calmer home.

Signs of a bored cat

Overgrooming or bald patches
Night-time zoomies and yowling
Scratching furniture, not the post
Chewing plants or cables
Sleeping much more than usual
Ambushing ankles

The simple formula

1
Hunt

Wand toy, stalk, chase, catch.

2
Eat

A treat or meal right after play.

3
Groom & sleep

Leave them to rest in peace.

Safe-home checklist

Window screens or safe latches
Toxic plants out of reach (lilies!)
Cords and hair ties tidied away
A hiding spot in every main room

Good to know

This planner is for everyday care and fun – not veterinary advice. A sudden change in behaviour, appetite or litter habits is a reason to call your vet.
