How the Kindness Elf works

Toddlers love little rituals. Each morning your child finds a note from their elf with one tiny, kind thing to do. You read it aloud, they do it with your help, and they color one circle on the tracker.

1
Choose your elf

Any small toy you already have can be the Kindness Elf: a teddy, a doll, a little figure. Fill in the elf passport together.

2
Print and cut

Print the notes on paper or cardstock and cut them apart (4 per page). Keep them in an envelope, ready for each morning.

3
One note a day

Put a note next to the elf in the evening. In the morning, read it together. Most missions take five minutes and cost nothing.

4
Celebrate

Color a circle on the tracker. On Christmas Eve, read the goodbye letter and give the "I was kind" certificate.

Forgot to move the elf?

Use the lifesaver letters: "I was running late" and "Someone touched me, but my magic is fine."

Make it yours

Skip any mission that doesn't fit your family. Use the blank notes and the 30 extra ideas.

30 more tiny kindness ideas

Swap in any of these when a note doesn't fit your day. Keep missions short, concrete and done together.

  • Pick up one piece of litter (with a grown-up)
  • Give your sibling the first turn
  • Say “please” at every meal today
  • Bring a grown-up a glass of water
  • Help feed the pet
  • Tidy up the bookshelf
  • Make a get-well drawing
  • Leave a sticker on someone’s pillow
  • Call a grandparent and sing
  • Give a compliment: “I like your hat!”
  • Help fold the towels
  • Put a toy back for someone else
  • Make a “thank you” picture for the mail carrier
  • Comfort a sad friend with a teddy
  • Help carry the laundry basket
  • Wash plastic cups in the sink
  • Share your snack
  • Draw a heart for the bus driver
  • Help make breakfast toast
  • Give the plants a drink
  • Clean up crayons without being asked
  • Say “sorry” and hug
  • Wave at a neighbor’s window
  • Make a necklace for a friend
  • Help match the socks
  • Let someone else choose the song
  • Brush the dog or the teddy
  • Help water the flowers outside
  • Give a good-night hug to everyone
