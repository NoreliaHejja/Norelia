For grown-ups

How to use this set

1
Pick the right letter

Ages 3–5: the picture letter (draw or glue wishes). Ages 5–7: the big-line letter. Ages 7–10: the full letter with a "kind thing I did this year" line.

2
Keep it calm

Ask for three wishes: something I want, something I need, something to share. It helps you shop and keeps the list short.

3
After Christmas

Use the thank-you notes for grandparents, teachers and friends. Little kids can draw, older kids can write.
