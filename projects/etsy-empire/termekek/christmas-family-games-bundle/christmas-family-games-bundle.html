Printable family game night · All ages

Christmas
Family Games

Seven easy games for Christmas Eve, family gatherings and school parties: bingo, a scavenger hunt, Would You Rather, charades, drawing, trivia and a score sheet. Print, cut and play.
13 pages · US Letter + A4
For grown-ups

How to play

1
Christmas Bingo

Give everyone a different card (8 included). Cut out the caller cards, shuffle and read them out. First to get five in a row shouts “JOLLY!”.

2
Scavenger Hunt

Set a timer for 10 minutes. Players find the items around the house and tick them off.

3
Would You Rather

Read a card, everyone picks a side of the room. Explaining why is half the fun.

4
Charades and Pictionary

Draw a card, act it out or draw it in one minute. No words, no letters.

5
Trivia

Read the questions; answers are on the last page. Play in teams of mixed ages.

Tip

Print the bingo cards on cardstock and use buttons, cereal or chocolate chips as markers.
