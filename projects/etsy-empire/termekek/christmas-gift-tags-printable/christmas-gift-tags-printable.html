Before you print

How to use your tags

1
Print

Use cardstock (160–250 g/m²) for sturdy tags, or plain paper for a quick fix. Print at 100% scale.

2
Cut

Cut along the dashed lines with scissors or a paper trimmer. Rounded corners are optional.

3
Punch and tie

Punch the small circle with a hole punch and tie with ribbon, twine or yarn.

4
Write

Fill in the To / From lines with a felt-tip pen. On dark tags, a white gel pen looks lovely.

Ink saver

The light styles (Ho Ho Ho, Joy & Love, Let It Snow) use far less ink than the full-colour red and green ones.
