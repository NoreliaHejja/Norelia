How to use the routine kit

Pre-readers follow pictures, not words. Put the chart where the routine happens – the bathroom door, the bedroom wall – and let your child color a dot for every step.

1
Pick the chart

Morning and bedtime charts with 8 steps each. Print a new one every week, or use a sheet protector.

2
Or build your own strip

Cut out the picture cards and stick them in order with tape or velcro dots. Swap cards as your routine changes.

3
Do it together for a week

Point at the next card instead of giving the next instruction. Less talking, fewer power struggles.

4
Celebrate

Color a star on the reward chart for each finished routine. Seven stars? Time for a small family treat.

Printing tips

US Letter and A4 PDFs included. Print the cards on card stock and laminate them for a routine that survives toddler hands.

Good to know

Every child is different. Use the steps that fit your family and skip the rest – this is a gentle planning tool, not parenting advice.

Make It Stick

Keep the same order every day – predictability is the magic.
Give a 5-minute warning before routines start.
Offer two small choices: "red pajamas or blue?"
Praise the effort, not the speed.
Lay out clothes the night before.
Use a song or timer for teeth brushing (2 minutes).
On hard days, do the routine together instead of skipping it.
