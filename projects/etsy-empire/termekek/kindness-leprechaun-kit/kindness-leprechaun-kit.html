How the Kindness Leprechaun works

From March 3 to 16, your child finds a note from the Kindness Leprechaun with one small, kind thing to do. After each one, color a gold coin on the tracker. On St. Patrick's Day, the leprechaun leaves a goodbye letter.

1
Meet your leprechaun

No special doll needed. A paper hat, a green sock or a little drawing can be your leprechaun. Fill in the passport together.

2
Print and cut

Print the notes on paper or cardstock and cut them apart (4 per page). Keep them in an envelope.

3
One note a day

Leave a note out each evening, read it together in the morning. Most missions take five minutes.

4
Celebrate

Color a coin on the tracker. On St. Patrick's Day, read the goodbye letter and give the "I was kind" certificate.

Forgot a note?

Use the lifesaver letters: "I got lost at the end of the rainbow" and "I was counting my gold".

Make it yours

Use the 4 gold coin cards as small rewards and the blank notes for your own ideas.

30 more tiny kindness ideas

Swap in any of these when a note doesn't fit your day. Keep missions short, concrete and done together.

  • Hold the door for someone
  • Say “I love you” at breakfast
  • Give your sibling the first turn
  • Say “please” at every meal
  • Bring a grown-up a glass of water
  • Help feed the pet
  • Make a get-well drawing
  • Call a grandparent and say “I love you”
  • Give a compliment: “I like your drawing!”
  • Help fold the towels
  • Put a toy back for someone else
  • Comfort a sad friend
  • Help carry the laundry basket
  • Wash plastic cups in the sink
  • Share your snack
  • Help make breakfast toast
  • Give the plants a drink
  • Clean up crayons without being asked
  • Say “sorry” and hug
  • Wave at a neighbor’s window
  • Make a bracelet for a friend
  • Help match the socks
  • Let someone else choose the song
  • Brush the dog or the teddy
  • Write a love note to a parent
  • Give a good-night hug to everyone
  • Plant flower seeds together
  • Make a bird feeder
  • Pick a flower for someone (with permission)
  • Clean up the garden toys
