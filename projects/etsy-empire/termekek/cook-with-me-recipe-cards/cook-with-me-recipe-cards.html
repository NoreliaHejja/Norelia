How to cook together

Pick a card, put it on the fridge, and let your child do the numbered jobs. Grown-up jobs are listed at the bottom of each card. Print on card stock and cut along the gaps – four cards per page.

LITTLE CHEF RULES
Wash hands first
Ask before you touch the oven
Knives are for grown-ups
Taste with a clean spoon
Spills are okay – we wipe together
Tie hair back, sleeves up
One job at a time
Clean up, then celebrate

Allergy check

Swap any ingredient your family avoids. Nut butters can be swapped for sunflower butter. Cut round foods like grapes in quarters for small children.

A note for parents

Always supervise. Oven, stove and knife steps are grown-up jobs. These are simple family recipes, not nutrition or medical advice.
