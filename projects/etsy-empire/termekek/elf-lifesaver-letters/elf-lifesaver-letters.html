How to use your elf letters

Print the pages, cut along the dashed lines, and keep the stack in a drawer. When a "help!" moment happens, grab the right note, add your child's name and leave it next to your elf. Works with any toy you already have.

Printing tips

Print on cream paper or card stock for a "real letter" feel. Write the names with a glitter pen. Fold in half and seal with a sticker.

Good to know

"Christmas Elf" is a generic name for this activity. Not affiliated with any elf doll brand: use any toy you have.

Elf survival plan

Set a phone alarm

A 9 p.m. "move the elf" reminder saves most mornings. If you still forget, letter #3 or #5 has you covered.

Keep it small

Kids remember the letters more than the big setups. A two-line note in a new spot is enough magic.

Plan your "big kid" moment

Letters #27 and #28 turn the end of the elf tradition into a proud promotion: your child becomes a magic-maker for younger siblings.
