How the Kindness Snowman works

January can feel long after the holidays. Each morning your child finds a note from the Kindness Snowman with one small, kind thing to do. Read it together, do it together, and color one snowflake on the tracker.

1
Meet your snowman

Any soft toy or a paper snowman can be your Kindness Snowman. Fill in the snowman passport together.

2
Print and cut

Print the notes on paper or cardstock and cut them apart (4 per page). Keep them in an envelope.

3
One note a day

Leave a note out in the evening, read it together in the morning. Most missions take five minutes and cost nothing.

4
Celebrate

Color a circle on the tracker. After 24 days, read the goodbye letter and give the "I was kind" certificate.

Forgot a note?

Use the lifesaver letters: "I was busy building snow forts" and "Too warm today!"

Make it yours

Start any day in January. Skip any mission that doesn't fit and use the blank notes and 30 extra ideas.

30 more tiny kindness ideas

Swap in any of these when a note doesn't fit your day. Keep missions short, concrete and done together.

  • Hold the door for someone
  • Help carry the groceries
  • Give your sibling the first turn
  • Say “please” at every meal
  • Bring a grown-up a glass of water
  • Help feed the pet
  • Tidy up the bookshelf
  • Make a get-well drawing
  • Leave a sticker on someone’s pillow
  • Call a grandparent and sing
  • Give a compliment: “I like your hat!”
  • Help fold the towels
  • Put a toy back for someone else
  • Make a picture for the school bus driver
  • Comfort a sad friend
  • Hang up your coat and someone else’s
  • Wash plastic cups in the sink
  • Share your snack
  • Help make breakfast toast
  • Give the plants a drink
  • Clean up crayons without being asked
  • Say “sorry” and hug
  • Wave at a neighbor’s window
  • Make a bracelet for a friend
  • Help match the socks
  • Let someone else choose the song
  • Brush the dog or the teddy
  • Fill the bird feeder
  • Warm up someone’s slippers
  • Give a good-night hug to everyone
