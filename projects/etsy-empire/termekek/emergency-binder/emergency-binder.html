How to use your binder

Nobody likes to think about emergencies. That is exactly why a plan helps: when you already wrote it down, you do not have to think in a stressful moment. Set aside one evening.

1
Fill it in together

Write in pencil so you can update it. Older kids can help with their own pages.

2
Keep it where you can grab it

Put the binder near your front door or with your emergency kit. Take a photo of each page so you also have it on your phone.

3
Practice once, twice a year

Walk to your meeting places with the kids. Check the kit, swap old water and batteries, update medicines and phone numbers.

4
Cut out the kids' cards

Fill in one wallet card per child and put it in their backpack.

Planning aid only

This binder helps you organize information. It is not medical, legal or emergency advice. In an emergency call 911 (US) or 112 (EU). For official guidance see Ready.gov or your local emergency management office.

Keep it private

Your binder holds personal data. Store it somewhere safe and shred old pages when you update.
