## Description
You love them more than anything. So why do you keep yelling?

If you've asked yourself that at 9 p.m., after the house finally went quiet, you are not a bad mother. You are a tired one, carrying more than one nervous system can comfortably hold. Yelling is rarely a lack of love. Most of the time it's an empty tank, a narrow window of patience and a handful of hard moments that happen every single day.

I Didn't Mean to Yell is a warm, practical guide for moms who want calmer days without pretending to be perfect. It is built on well-established approaches such as self-compassion, emotion coaching, attachment-based "rupture and repair" and simple tools for calming the nervous system, explained in plain language.

Inside you'll find:
- The Two-Breath Pause: one tool you can try tonight
- Why loving moms yell, and how to widen your window of patience
- Your Trigger Map: the times and places your fuse is shortest
- Repair after yelling, with word-for-word scripts for ages 2–4, 5–7 and 8–12
- Calm, firm limits without raising your voice
- Step-by-step plans for mornings, bedtime, sibling fights, screens, big feelings and back-talk
- Ten hard moments walked through, from supermarket meltdowns to homework battles
- Self-compassion instead of shame, and the echoes of your own childhood
- How to get your partner and the grandparents on the same page
- When it's more than yelling: signs to get extra help, with helpline numbers
- A 30-Day Calm Mom Plan, a fridge card and a complete script library
- A Quick Finder so you can open the book at exactly the page you need

Imagine a bedtime where you're the calm one. Start with one page tonight.

This book is not a substitute for professional help. If you are in crisis, please contact your local emergency number or a crisis line.
