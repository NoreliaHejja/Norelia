## Description
Winter break is supposed to be restful, so why does it so often turn into long days of screens, meltdowns and "I'm bored"?

This warm, practical, guilt-free guide shows parents how to protect children from digital noise during school holidays without banning technology or turning into the screen police.

Inside:
- Why screens are so hard to put down, explained simply (and how to explain it to kids)
- A step-by-step family break plan, set up before the first day
- Four screen approaches that work, from screen windows to a full family detox
- Scripts for the hardest moment: when the screen goes off
- A simple daily rhythm and fun theme days
- How to turn boredom into creativity, plus the "I'm bored" jar
- 100 screen-free games and activities: active indoor games, outdoor winter fun, kitchen projects, crafts, pretend play, board and card games, quiet activities, kitchen science and family kindness
- A family agreement, daily checklist, screen time tracker and memory pages

For parents of kids ages 2–12. A calmer, more connected break starts here.
