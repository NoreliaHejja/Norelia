## Description
"Five more minutes." Then the meltdown. Then the guilt.

If school breaks in your home slowly turn into long days of tablets, tears and "I'm bored," you are not a bad parent, and your children are not broken. Many apps and games are built to be hard to put down, and holidays remove the structure that usually keeps them in check.

Give Them Back Their Wonder is a warm, practical, guilt-free guide to a calmer, more connected school break, because children don't need fewer joys; they need different ones.

Inside you'll find:
- Why screens are so hard to put down, explained simply (and how to explain it to your kids)
- What to expect in the first days, and why it gets easier after day three
- A family break plan you set up before day one, with four screen approaches that work
- Scripts for the hardest moment: when the screen goes off
- What works at every age: toddlers, early school years, tweens
- "Good screen time" that brings families together
- A survival plan for working parents
- How to handle grandparents' and relatives' rules, and travel days without the tablet
- 100 screen-free games and activities, plus 20 family favorites explained step by step
- A sample 14-day break plan, printable-style charts and memory pages

Picture it: a blanket fort, flour on the counter, your child laughing at a game you invented together. Start with one game tonight.

This book is not a substitute for professional advice.
