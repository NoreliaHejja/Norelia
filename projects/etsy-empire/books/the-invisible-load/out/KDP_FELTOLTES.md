## Description
It's 10:40 at night. The house is finally quiet, and your mind starts reading out a list nobody else is keeping: the permission slip, the dentist, the birthday present, the milk, your mother's medication.

If you're tired in a way that sleep doesn't fix, you may be carrying the invisible load: the noticing, planning, remembering and checking that keeps a family running. It's real work. It's just the kind nobody sees.

Who Takes Care of Mom? is a warm, practical 30-day reset for women who are done being the household manager. No guilt, no complicated system, no blame.

Inside you'll find:
- A twenty-minute brain dump you can do tonight
- What the invisible load really is, and how to explain it in one minute
- The quiet signs of burnout, and when to see a doctor
- A simple family inventory that makes the hidden work visible
- Word-for-word scripts for the conversation with your partner
- How to hand over whole jobs, not tasks (and why you must stop reminding)
- What to say when he says "just tell me what to do"
- Age-by-age chores so kids can carry more
- Systems that think for you: weekly look-ahead, meals on repeat, one shared calendar
- How to let go of "my way" and rest without earning it
- A chapter for single moms carrying it alone
- Holidays, birthdays and big changes, plus words for grandparents, schools and other parents
- A fridge card and a day-by-day 30-Day Reset

Start tonight with one page and a pen. Feel lighter by the weekend.

This book is not a substitute for medical or psychological advice.
