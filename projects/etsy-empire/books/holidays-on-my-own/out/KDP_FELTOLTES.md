## Description
"Everyone has someone. Except me."

If that quiet thought finds you every December, while the lights go up, the couples' photos roll in and your family asks the same question again, this book was written for you.

There is nothing wrong with you. The holidays turn the volume up on everything: loneliness, grief, comparison, old memories. And because nobody hands single women a plan for this season, the hardest days often arrive unprepared.

Still Lit is that plan: warm, honest and practical. Grounded in gentle, well-established approaches such as self-compassion, reframing unhelpful thoughts and planning small, meaningful activities, it helps you:

- Name what you feel, because named feelings lose their grip
- Step out of the social media comparison trap
- Answer "So, are you seeing anyone?" with calm, ready-to-use words
- Map your hardest days, and give each one an anchor
- Start rituals for one that feel meaningful, not sad
- Get through the first holidays after a breakup, divorce or loss
- Plan Christmas Eve, Christmas Day and New Year's Eve hour by hour
- Recognize when it's more than the holiday blues, and where to get help

Includes worksheets, a library of scripts, 30 cozy ideas for solo evenings, 24 Days of Gentle Kindness and journal pages.

You can't change the season. But you can change how it feels from the inside. Start with one page tonight. The light is still on.

This book is not a substitute for professional help.
