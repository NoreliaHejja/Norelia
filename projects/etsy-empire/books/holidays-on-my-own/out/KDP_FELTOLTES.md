## Description
The holidays can be the loudest time of year to be on your own. Whether you are newly single, divorced, widowed, far from family or simply tired of a season built around couples, this gentle, practical guide will help you get through December with your heart intact, and maybe even enjoy it.

Inside you will find:
- How to name and understand holiday feelings, from loneliness and grief to relief
- A plan for stepping out of the social media comparison trap
- Ready-to-use scripts for family gatherings and "So, are you seeing anyone?"
- A holiday map to plan your hardest days, with an anchor for each one
- New rituals for one, and ideas for single moms on child-free days
- Low-pressure ways to build connection
- An hour-by-hour plan for Christmas Eve, Christmas Day and New Year's Eve
- Simple body-first habits for sleep, food, movement and light
- Money and gift boundaries
- Clear signs of when it is more than the holiday blues, and where to get help
- 24 Days of Gentle Kindness, reflection prompts and journal pages

Warm, honest and practical: a companion for the season, not homework.
