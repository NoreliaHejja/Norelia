Round Crochet Coaster – Crochet Pattern

Your first circle: only single crochet and increases. About 30 minutes.
Skill level1/6 – Beginner
Finished sizeapprox. 10 cm (4 in) across
Hook4 mm
GaugeNot critical; work tightly so it lies flat.

You will need

  • Worsted cotton yarn, about 15 g, any colour
  • 4 mm hook, tapestry needle, scissors

Notes

  • Each round is joined; the ch 1 at the start does not count as a stitch.

Coaster

Round 1
Magic ring, ch 1, 6 sc into the ring. Pull tight, join with a sl st.
(6 sts)
Round 2
Ch 1. Inc in each st around. Join with a sl st to the first sc.
(12 sts)
Round 3
Ch 1. *Sc in the next 1 sts, inc in the next st.* Repeat from * to * 5 more times. Join with a sl st to the first sc.
(18 sts)
Round 4
Ch 1. *Sc in the next 2 sts, inc in the next st.* Repeat from * to * 5 more times. Join with a sl st to the first sc.
(24 sts)
Round 5
Ch 1. *Sc in the next 3 sts, inc in the next st.* Repeat from * to * 5 more times. Join with a sl st to the first sc.
(30 sts)
Round 6
Ch 1. *Sc in the next 4 sts, inc in the next st.* Repeat from * to * 5 more times. Join with a sl st to the first sc.
(36 sts)
Round 7
Edging: sl st loosely in each st around. Fasten off.
(36 sts)

Finishing

  • Weave in ends. If it ruffles, wet it and dry flat.
