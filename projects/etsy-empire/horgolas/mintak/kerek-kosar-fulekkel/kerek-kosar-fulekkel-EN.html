Round Basket with Handles – Crochet Pattern

Flat base, back-loop turning round, moss-stitch sides and handles. About 4 hours.
Skill level6/6 – Advanced beginner+
Finished sizeapprox. 18 cm (7 in) across, 12 cm (4.75 in) tall
Hook6 mm
Gauge12 sc = 10 cm with yarn held double. If the base ruffles, go down a hook size.

You will need

  • Chunky cotton or T-shirt yarn, about 250 g, or worsted yarn held double
  • 6 mm hook, stitch marker, tapestry needle

Notes

  • Base rounds are joined. In moss-stitch rounds the count is 30 sc + 30 ch = 60.

Base

Round 1
Magic ring, ch 1, 6 sc. Join with a sl st.
(6 sts)
Round 2
Ch 1. Inc in each st around. Join with a sl st to the first sc.
(12 sts)
Round 3
Ch 1. *Sc in the next 1 sts, inc in the next st.* Repeat from * to * 5 more times. Join with a sl st to the first sc.
(18 sts)
Round 4
Ch 1. *Sc in the next 2 sts, inc in the next st.* Repeat from * to * 5 more times. Join with a sl st to the first sc.
(24 sts)
Round 5
Ch 1. *Sc in the next 3 sts, inc in the next st.* Repeat from * to * 5 more times. Join with a sl st to the first sc.
(30 sts)
Round 6
Ch 1. *Sc in the next 4 sts, inc in the next st.* Repeat from * to * 5 more times. Join with a sl st to the first sc.
(36 sts)
Round 7
Ch 1. *Sc in the next 5 sts, inc in the next st.* Repeat from * to * 5 more times. Join with a sl st to the first sc.
(42 sts)
Round 8
Ch 1. *Sc in the next 6 sts, inc in the next st.* Repeat from * to * 5 more times. Join with a sl st to the first sc.
(48 sts)
Round 9
Ch 1. *Sc in the next 7 sts, inc in the next st.* Repeat from * to * 5 more times. Join with a sl st to the first sc.
(54 sts)
Round 10
Ch 1. *Sc in the next 8 sts, inc in the next st.* Repeat from * to * 5 more times. Join with a sl st to the first sc.
(60 sts)

Sides

Round 11
Ch 1, sc in the back loop only of each st (this turns the side up). Join.
(60 sts)
Rounds 12–21
Ch 1, *sc, ch 1, skip 1* around; on later rounds work each sc into the ch-1 space of the round below. Join with a sl st.
(60 sts)

Handles and edge

Round 22
Ch 1, sc 10, ch 10, skip 10, sc 20, ch 10, skip 10, sc 10. Join.
(60 sts)
Round 23
Ch 1, sc in each st, 10 sc into each ch-10 space. Join.
(60 sts)
Round 24
Reverse sc (crab stitch) or sl st in each st around. Fasten off.
(60 sts)

Finishing

  • Weave in ends on the inside.
