Mini Christmas Stocking Ornament – Crochet Pattern

Two flat pieces in single crochet, joined and finished with a white cuff and a hanging loop. About 1 hour.
Skill level2/6 – Easy
Finished sizeapprox. 6.5 cm wide × 10 cm tall (2.5 × 4 in) with worsted yarn
Hook4 mm (US G/6)
GaugeNot critical. Keep stitches firm.

You will need

  • Worsted / aran weight cotton or acrylic yarn: red (about 8 g), white (3 g)
  • 4 mm crochet hook, tapestry needle, scissors
  • Optional: a small bell or a mini candy cane to tuck inside

Notes

  • Start: Row 1 is the top edge of the leg; you work downwards and the foot grows to one side. Written in US terms; a UK table is above.
  • The number in brackets is the stitch count at the end of the row. The turning ch 1 does not count as a stitch.
  • Bigger stocking: use chunky yarn and a 5–6 mm hook; the same rows give about 9 × 14 cm.

Diagrams: finished shape and stitch chart

Finished shape
Stocking piece, worked top down: red = increases at the toe edge, blue = decreases

Stocking piece (red, make 2)

Row 1
Ch 9. Sc in 2nd ch from hook and in each ch across. Turn. This is the top of the leg.
(8 sts)
Rows 2–10
Ch 1 (does not count as a st), sc in each st across. Turn. (9 rows)
(8 sts)
Row 11
Ch 1, sc in the next 7 sts, 2 sc in the last st. Turn. The increases always go on the same edge: this edge becomes the toe.
(9 sts)
Row 12
Ch 1, 2 sc in the first st (toe edge), sc in each st across. Turn.
(10 sts)
Row 13
Ch 1, sc in the next 9 sts, 2 sc in the last st. Turn.
(11 sts)
Row 14
Ch 1, 2 sc in the first st, sc in each st across. Turn.
(12 sts)
Rows 15–16
Ch 1, sc in each st across. Turn. (2 rows)
(12 sts)
Row 17
Ch 1, sc2tog, sc in the next 8 sts, sc2tog. Turn. This rounds the heel and the toe.
(10 sts)
Row 18
Ch 1, sc2tog, sc in the next 6 sts, sc2tog. Fasten off. Make a second piece exactly the same.
(8 sts)

Joining

Edge
Lay piece 2 exactly on top of piece 1 with both toes pointing the same way. With red, sc through both layers along the back edge, around the heel, along the sole and around the toe and up the front edge, leaving the top (rows 1) open. Work 3 sc into each corner of the toe. Fasten off.

Cuff (white)

Round 1
With white, join at the back top corner. Sc in each st along the top of both pieces (8 + 8). Join with a sl st.
(16 sts)
Rounds 2–3
Ch 1, sc in each st around. Join with a sl st. (2 rounds)
(16 sts)
Loop
Ch 12, sl st into the same st at the back corner. Fasten off and weave in.

Finishing

  • Weave in all ends on the inside.
  • Optional: sew a small bell to the cuff at round 2 of the cuff (not for children under 3).
  • Happy hooking!
