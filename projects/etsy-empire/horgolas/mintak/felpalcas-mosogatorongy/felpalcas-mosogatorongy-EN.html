Half Double Crochet Dishcloth – Crochet Pattern

Straight rows of half double crochet with a border. About 1.5 hours.
Skill level2/6 – Beginner
Finished sizeapprox. 20 × 20 cm (8 × 8 in)
Hook4,5 mm
Gauge15 hdc = 10 cm (4 in)

You will need

  • Worsted 100% cotton yarn, about 45 g
  • 4.5 mm hook, tapestry needle, scissors

Abbreviations (US terms)

Notes

  • The ch 2 at the start of a row does not count as a stitch.

Dishcloth

Row 1
Ch 32. Hdc in the 3rd ch from hook and in each ch across. Turn.
(30 sts)
Rows 2–22
Ch 2, hdc in each st across. Turn.
(30 sts)

Border

Border
Ch 1, sc evenly around: 1 sc in each row end on the sides, 3 sc in each corner. Join with a sl st and fasten off.

Finishing

  • Weave in ends. Washable at 40 °C.
