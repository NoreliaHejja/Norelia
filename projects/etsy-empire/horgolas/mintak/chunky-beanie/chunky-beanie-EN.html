Chunky Ribbed Beanie – Crochet Pattern (Adult S/M/L)

Worked top down in half double crochet with a stretchy post-stitch brim. A quick gift in about 2 hours.
Skill level3/6 – Easy+
Finished sizeS head 50–53 cm · M 54–57 cm · L 58–61 cm; about 21–23 cm tall
Hook8 mm (US L/11)
Gauge9 hdc = 10 cm (4 in) in rounds. Check it: a bigger gauge makes a bigger hat.

You will need

  • Chunky / bulky weight yarn (#5), about 100 g (S) to 130 g (L), e.g. wool blend or acrylic
  • 8 mm hook, stitch marker, tapestry needle; optional faux fur pom-pom

Notes

  • Start: the magic ring is the top of the crown; you work downwards in joined rounds. The ch 2 at the start of a round does not count as a stitch.
  • Sizes: all sizes work rounds 1–7. Size M adds round 8, size L adds rounds 8 and 9. Then everyone works the body and the brim.
  • Front post dc / back post dc: a double crochet worked around the post of the stitch below, from the front or from the back. This makes the stretchy rib.
  • Longer slouchy beanie: work 3–4 more body rounds before the brim.

Diagrams: finished shape and stitch chart

Finished shape (flat width S–L)
Crown rounds 1–9 (S stops at 7, M at 8)

Crown (all sizes)

Round 1
Magic ring, ch 2 (does not count as a st), 6 hdc into the ring. Join with a sl st to the first hdc.
(6 sts)
Round 2
Ch 2, 2 hdc in each st around. Join.
(12 sts)
Round 3
Ch 2, *hdc in the next 1 st, 2 hdc in the next st.* Repeat 6 times. Join.
(18 sts)
Round 4
Ch 2, *hdc in the next 2 sts, 2 hdc in the next st.* Repeat 6 times. Join.
(24 sts)
Round 5
Ch 2, *hdc in the next 3 sts, 2 hdc in the next st.* Repeat 6 times. Join.
(30 sts)
Round 6
Ch 2, *hdc in the next 4 sts, 2 hdc in the next st.* Repeat 6 times. Join.
(36 sts)
Round 7
Ch 2, *hdc in the next 5 sts, 2 hdc in the next st.* Repeat 6 times. Join.
(42 sts)

Size M and L

Round 8
Size M and L only: ch 2, *hdc in the next 6 sts, 2 hdc in the next st.* Repeat 6 times. Join. (Size S: skip to the body.)
(48 sts)

Size L

Round 9
Size L only: ch 2, *hdc in the next 7 sts, 2 hdc in the next st.* Repeat 6 times. Join.
(54 sts)

Body and brim (all sizes)

Body
Ch 2, hdc in each st around, join. Work 8 rounds even: S 42 sts, M 48 sts, L 54 sts. Try the hat on: it should reach about 3 cm above the ears.
Brim (4 rounds)
Ch 2, *front post dc around the next st, back post dc around the next st* around. Join. Repeat for 4 rounds, keeping fronts over fronts. Fasten off.

Finishing

  • Weave in the ends on the inside.
  • Optional: attach a pom-pom to the centre of round 1 (the magic ring).
  • Happy hooking!
