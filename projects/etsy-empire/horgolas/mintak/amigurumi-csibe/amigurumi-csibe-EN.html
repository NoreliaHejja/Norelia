Amigurumi Chick – Crochet Pattern

One-piece figure with beak, wings and tuft; practise assembly. About 2 hours.
Skill level5/6 – Intermediate
Finished sizeapprox. 7 cm (2.75 in) tall
Hook3,5 mm
GaugeWork tightly.

You will need

  • Worsted yarn: yellow (15 g), orange (2 g)
  • Two 6 mm safety eyes (or black yarn to embroider), fibrefill, 3.5 mm hook, marker, tapestry needle

Notes

  • Worked in a continuous spiral; do not join rounds. Use a stitch marker for the first stitch.
  • For children under 3, embroider the eyes instead of using safety eyes.

Diagrams: finished shape and stitch chart

Finished shape and assembly
Body rounds 1–4 from above (increases)

Body (yellow)

Round 1
Magic ring, ch 1, 6 sc into the ring. Pull tight.
(6 sts)
Round 2
Inc in each st around.
(12 sts)
Round 3
*Sc in the next 1 sts, inc in the next st.* Repeat 6 times.
(18 sts)
Round 4
*Sc in the next 2 sts, inc in the next st.* Repeat 6 times.
(24 sts)
Rounds 5–10
Sc in each st around (6 rounds). Insert the eyes between rounds 8 and 9, 5 sts apart.
(24 sts)
Round 11
*Sc in the next 2 sts, sc2tog.* Repeat 6 times.
(18 sts)
Round 12
*Sc in the next st, sc2tog.* Repeat 6 times. Stuff the body.
(12 sts)
Round 13
Sc2tog around. Close the opening.
(6 sts)

Beak (orange)

Round 1
Magic ring, 4 sc.
(4 sts)
Round 2
*Sc in the next st, inc.* Repeat twice. Fasten off with a long tail; do not stuff.
(6 sts)

Wing (yellow, make 2)

Round 1
Magic ring, 6 sc.
(6 sts)
Round 2
*Sc in the next st, inc.* Repeat 3 times. Fold flat, fasten off with a long tail.
(9 sts)

Finishing

  • Sew the beak between the eyes, one round lower.
  • Sew the wings to the sides at round 11.
  • Tuft: pull 3 strands of yellow through the top of the head, knot and trim to 1.5 cm.
