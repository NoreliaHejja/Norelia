Easy Crochet Heart Applique – Crochet Pattern

A one-round heart for cards, garlands and Valentine's gifts. About 10 minutes.
Skill levelEasy
Finished sizeapprox. 5 cm (2 in) with DK cotton
Hook3.5 mm (US E/4)
GaugeNot critical.

You will need

  • Light worsted / DK cotton yarn: red or pink (about 3 g)
  • 3.5 mm crochet hook, tapestry needle, scissors

Notes

  • Everything is worked into the magic ring. Ch 3 at the start counts as the first dc.

Diagrams: finished shape and stitch chart

Chart: every stitch goes into the magic ring

Heart

Round 1
Make a magic ring. Into the ring work: ch 3 (counts as dc), 3 dc, 3 hdc, ch 1, 1 dc (this is the point), ch 1, 3 hdc, 3 dc, ch 3, sl st into the ring. (14 sts, not counting the ch-1s)
(14 sts)
Shaping
Pull the ring tail tight until the top of the heart dips in the middle. Fasten off.

Finishing

  • Weave in the ends.
  • Glue onto a card, sew onto a bag, or string several onto a garland.
