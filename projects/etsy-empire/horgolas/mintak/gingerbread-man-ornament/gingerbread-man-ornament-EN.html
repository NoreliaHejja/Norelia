Mini Gingerbread Man Ornament – Crochet Pattern

Flat pieces in single crochet (head, body, arms, legs) sewn together, with white yarn details. About 1.5 hours.
Skill level2/6 – Easy
Finished sizeapprox. 9 cm wide × 11 cm tall (3.5 × 4.3 in) with worsted yarn
Hook4 mm (US G/6)
GaugeNot critical. Keep stitches firm.

You will need

  • Worsted / aran weight cotton or acrylic yarn: light brown (about 10 g), white (2 g) for details; optional red for a bow
  • 4 mm hook, tapestry needle, scissors

Notes

  • Start: make all pieces first (head, body, 2 arms, 2 legs), then sew them together. Row 1 of the body is the neck.
  • Leave a long tail on every piece for sewing. The turning ch 1 does not count as a stitch.

Diagrams: finished shape and stitch chart

Finished shape and assembly
Head: 3 rounds
Body: 6 rows of 6 sc

Head (brown)

Round 1
Magic ring, ch 1, 6 sc into the ring. Join with a sl st.
(6 sts)
Round 2
Ch 1, 2 sc in each st around. Join.
(12 sts)
Round 3
Ch 1, *sc in the next st, 2 sc in the next st.* Repeat 6 times. Join. Fasten off with a long tail.
(18 sts)

Body (brown)

Row 1
Ch 7. Sc in 2nd ch from hook and in each ch across. Turn.
(6 sts)
Rows 2–6
Ch 1, sc in each st across. Turn. (5 rows) Fasten off with a long tail.
(6 sts)

Arm (brown, make 2)

Row 1
Ch 6. Sc in 2nd ch from hook and in each ch across. Turn.
(5 sts)
Row 2
Ch 1, sc in each st across. Fasten off with a long tail.
(5 sts)

Leg (brown, make 2)

Row 1
Ch 7. Sc in 2nd ch from hook and in each ch across. Turn.
(6 sts)
Row 2
Ch 1, sc in each st across. Fasten off with a long tail.
(6 sts)

Finishing

  • Sew the head to row 1 of the body, centred, overlapping the body by about 0.5 cm.
  • Sew one arm to each side of the body at rows 1–2, pointing slightly down.
  • Sew the legs to row 6 of the body: one at the left edge, one at the right edge.
  • With white yarn, embroider two eyes and a smile on round 2 of the head, two buttons on rows 2 and 4 of the body, and a zigzag line across the end of each arm and leg.
  • Hanging loop: ch 12 at the top of round 3 of the head and sl st back into the same st. Happy hooking!
