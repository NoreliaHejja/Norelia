Amigurumi Ball – Crochet Pattern

Spiral, increase, decrease and stuffing: amigurumi basics. About 1.5 hours.
Skill level4/6 – Intermediate
Finished sizeapprox. 8 cm (3 in) across
Hook3,5 mm
GaugeWork tightly so the stuffing doesn't show.

You will need

  • Worsted cotton or acrylic yarn, about 20 g
  • 3.5 mm hook, stitch marker, fibrefill (about 15 g), tapestry needle

Abbreviations (US terms)

UKHU
scsingle crochetdcrp
sc2togsingle crochet 2 together (decrease)dc2togfogy
inc2 sc in the same stitch (increase)incszap
MRmagic ringMRvgy
st(s)stitch(es)st(s)szem

Notes

  • Worked in a continuous spiral; do not join rounds. Use a stitch marker for the first stitch.

Ball

Round 1
Magic ring, ch 1, 6 sc into the ring. Pull tight.
(6 sts)
Round 2
Inc in each st around.
(12 sts)
Round 3
*Sc in the next 1 sts, inc in the next st.* Repeat 6 times.
(18 sts)
Round 4
*Sc in the next 2 sts, inc in the next st.* Repeat 6 times.
(24 sts)
Round 5
*Sc in the next 3 sts, inc in the next st.* Repeat 6 times.
(30 sts)
Round 6
*Sc in the next 4 sts, inc in the next st.* Repeat 6 times.
(36 sts)
Rounds 7–12
Sc in each st around (6 rounds).
(36 sts)
Round 13
*Sc in the next 4 sts, sc2tog.* Repeat 6 times.
(30 sts)
Round 14
*Sc in the next 3 sts, sc2tog.* Repeat 6 times.
(24 sts)
Round 15
*Sc in the next 2 sts, sc2tog.* Repeat 6 times.
(18 sts)
Stuffing
Stuff the ball firmly.
Round 16
*Sc in the next 1 sts, sc2tog.* Repeat 6 times.
(12 sts)
Round 17
Sc2tog around.
(6 sts)

Finishing

  • Thread the tail through the front loops of the last 6 sts, pull tight and weave in. For under 3s, make sure everything is sewn securely.
