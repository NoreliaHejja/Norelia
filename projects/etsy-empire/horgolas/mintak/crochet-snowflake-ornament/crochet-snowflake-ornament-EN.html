Easy Crochet Snowflake Ornament – Crochet Pattern

A quick 3-round snowflake for your tree, gift tags or window. About 30 minutes.
Skill levelEasy
Finished sizeapprox. 9 cm (3.5 in) across with DK cotton
Hook3.5 mm (US E/4)
GaugeNot critical.

You will need

  • Light worsted / DK cotton yarn: white (about 5 g)
  • 3.5 mm crochet hook, tapestry needle, scissors
  • Optional: starch spray to stiffen, ribbon to hang

Notes

  • Written in US crochet terms. A ch-5 space is the gap made by 5 chains.

Snowflake (white)

Round 1
Make a magic ring. Ch 1, 12 sc into the ring. Pull tight and join with a sl st to the first sc.
(12 sts)
Round 2
Ch 1. *Sc in the next st, ch 5, skip the next st.* Repeat from * to * 5 more times. Join with a sl st to the first sc. (6 sc and 6 ch-5 spaces)
(6 sts)
Round 3
Sl st into the first ch-5 space. *In the ch-5 space work (3 sc, ch 3, 3 sc); sl st in the next sc of Round 2.* Repeat from * to * 5 more times. Fasten off. (6 points, 36 sc)
(36 sts)

Finishing

  • Weave in the ends.
  • Pin the snowflake flat on a towel, open each point, spray with starch and let it dry.
  • Thread a ribbon through one point to hang.
