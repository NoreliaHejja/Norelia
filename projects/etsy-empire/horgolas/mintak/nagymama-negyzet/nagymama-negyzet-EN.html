Classic Granny Square – Crochet Pattern

Dc clusters and corners: the base of blankets and bags. About 40 minutes.
Skill level3/6 – Easy
Finished sizeapprox. 12 × 12 cm (4 rounds)
Hook4 mm
GaugeNot critical.

You will need

  • Worsted yarn, 2–4 colours, about 15 g in total
  • 4 mm hook, tapestry needle, scissors

Notes

  • Ch 3 at the start counts as a dc. Stitch counts are dc only. You may change colour at the end of any round.

Square

Round 1
Magic ring. Ch 3 (counts as dc), 2 dc, ch 2, *3 dc, ch 2* 2 more times. Join with a sl st to the top of ch 3.
(12 sts)
Round 2
Sl st into the first corner space. Ch 3, 2 dc, ch 2, 3 dc in the same space (corner). In every other corner space: 3 dc, ch 2, 3 dc. Ch 1 between the corner groups. Join with a sl st.
(24 sts)
Round 3
Sl st into the first corner space. Ch 3, 2 dc, ch 2, 3 dc in the same space (corner). In every other corner space: 3 dc, ch 2, 3 dc. Ch 1 between all groups; along each side work 3 dc in every ch-1 space (1 group(s) per side). Join with a sl st.
(36 sts)
Round 4
Sl st into the first corner space. Ch 3, 2 dc, ch 2, 3 dc in the same space (corner). In every other corner space: 3 dc, ch 2, 3 dc. Ch 1 between all groups; along each side work 3 dc in every ch-1 space (2 group(s) per side). Join with a sl st.
(48 sts)

Finishing

  • Weave in ends and block flat.
