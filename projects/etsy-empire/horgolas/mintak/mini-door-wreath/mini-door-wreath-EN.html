Mini Christmas Door Wreath – Crochet Pattern

A flat little wreath with a bow and berries for your door, window or tree. About 1.5 hours.
Skill levelBeginner
Finished sizeapprox. 12 cm (4.75 in) across with worsted yarn
Hook4 mm (US G/6)
GaugeNot critical. Keep stitches firm so the wreath lies flat.

You will need

  • Worsted / aran weight yarn: green (about 15 g), red (5 g)
  • 4 mm hook, tapestry needle, scissors
  • Optional: a ring or ribbon for hanging

Notes

  • Written in US crochet terms. The number in brackets is the stitch count at the end of the round.
  • If the wreath starts to ruffle, go down a hook size; if it cups, go up a size.

Wreath (green)

Round 1
With green, ch 30 and join with a sl st to the first ch to form a ring (don't twist). Ch 1, sc in each ch around. Join with a sl st to the first sc.
(30 sts)
Round 2
Ch 1. *Sc in the next 4 sts, inc in the next st.* Repeat from * to * 5 more times. Join with a sl st to the first sc.
(36 sts)
Round 3
Ch 1. *Sc in the next 5 sts, inc in the next st.* Repeat from * to * 5 more times. Join with a sl st to the first sc.
(42 sts)
Round 4
Ch 1. *Sc in the next 6 sts, inc in the next st.* Repeat from * to * 5 more times. Join with a sl st to the first sc.
(48 sts)
Round 5
Ch 1. Sc in each st around, working in the front loop only for a leafy edge. Join and fasten off.
(48 sts)

Bow (red)

Row 1
Ch 21. Sc in 2nd ch from hook and in each ch across. Turn.
(20 sts)
Rows 2–3
Ch 1, sc in each st across. Turn. Fasten off after Row 3, leaving a long tail.
(20 sts)
Shaping
Fold both short ends to the middle and wrap the long tail tightly around the centre 6–8 times to make a bow.

Berries (red, make 3)

Round 1
Make a magic ring, ch 1, 6 sc into the ring. Pull tight, join and fasten off. Weave the tail through the stitches and pull to make a little ball.
(6 sts)

Finishing

  • Weave in the ends of the wreath.
  • Sew the bow to the bottom or top of the wreath and the berries next to it.
  • Add a loop of yarn or ribbon for hanging. Not a toy for children under 3 (small parts).
