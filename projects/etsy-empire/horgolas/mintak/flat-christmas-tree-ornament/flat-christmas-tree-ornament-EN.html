Flat Christmas Tree Ornament – Crochet Pattern

A quick, beginner-friendly tree with a little star. About 1 hour.
Skill levelBeginner
Finished sizeapprox. 7 cm wide × 9 cm tall (2.75 × 3.5 in) with worsted yarn
Hook4 mm (US G/6)
GaugeNot critical. Keep stitches firm.

You will need

  • Worsted / aran weight cotton or acrylic yarn: green (about 10 g), brown (2 g), yellow (2 g)
  • 4 mm crochet hook, tapestry needle, scissors
  • Optional: small buttons or beads as baubles, ribbon for hanging

Row 14
Ch 1. Sc3tog over the remaining 3 sts. Fasten off, leaving a long tail.
(1 sts)

Trunk (brown)

Work into the bottom edge of Row 1 (the foundation chain side), centered.
Row 1
Skip 6 sts from the right edge, join brown with a sl st in the next st. Ch 1, sc in the same st and in the next 2 sts. Turn.
(3 sts)
Rows 2–3
Ch 1, sc in each st across. Turn. Fasten off after Row 3.
(3 sts)

Star (yellow)

Round 1
Make a magic ring. Ch 1, 5 sc into the ring. Pull tight and join with a sl st to the first sc.
(5 sts)
Round 2
*Ch 3, sl st in the 2nd ch from hook, hdc in the next ch, sl st in the next sc of Round 1.* Repeat from * to * 4 more times. Fasten off, leaving a tail for sewing.
(5 sts)

Finishing

  • Weave in all ends except the long green tail at the top.
  • Sew the star to the top point of the tree.
  • Optional: sew on small buttons or beads as baubles (not for children under 3).
  • Use the green tail to make a hanging loop, or thread a ribbon through the top.
