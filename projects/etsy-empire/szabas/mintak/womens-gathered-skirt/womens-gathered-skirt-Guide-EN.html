Before you start

  • The easiest skirt there is: two rectangles, an elastic casing and a hem. No zip, no darts. Length 60 cm (23.5 in) plus casing.
  • No seam allowance included: add 1.5 cm (5/8 in) to the side seams and 3 cm to the hem.
  • Good fabrics: cotton lawn, poplin, viscose, linen, double gauze.

Printing

  1. Print at 100% / actual size and check the test square on page A1.
  2. Tape the pages together, or print the A0 file at a copy shop. Tip: you can also draw the rectangle straight onto the fabric with the measurements below.

Choosing your size

Choose by your hip: the total width is your hip + 4 cm, times 1.5, so the skirt slides over the hips and gathers softly. Cut the elastic to your waist minus 5 cm (XS 57 · S 63 · M 69 · L 77 · XL 85 · 2X 93 · 3X 101 cm).

You will need

  • Fabric: 1.4 m (1.5 yd) of 140 cm (55 in) wide fabric for XS–L, 1.8 m (2 yd) for XL–3X
  • 3 cm (1.25 in) wide soft knit elastic, safety pin

Sewing steps

  1. Cut 2 panels on the fold.
  2. Sew both side seams and finish the edges.
  3. Fold the top edge down 1 cm, then again along the casing fold line. Stitch close to the edge, leaving a 5 cm gap.
  4. Thread the elastic through with a safety pin, overlap the ends 2 cm and sew them together. Close the gap.
  5. Spread the gathers evenly, try it on, then sew a 3 cm double-fold hem.

Make it yours

  • Add patch pockets, a tie belt or a ruffle at the hem.
  • For a fuller skirt use fullness 2 (width = hip + 4 cm, times 2).
  • Kids' version: use the same method with the child's hip measurement.
