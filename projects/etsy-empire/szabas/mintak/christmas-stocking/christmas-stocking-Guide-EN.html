S is a small ornament or gift-card stocking, M is the classic fireplace size, L holds big gifts. Cuff = 2 × leg width, 16 cm tall before folding.

Before you start

  • A lined Christmas stocking with a folded cuff and a hanging loop. Beginner friendly: straight seams and one curve. About 1 hour.
  • No seam allowance included: add 1 cm (3/8 in) on all edges.
  • Fabrics: cotton, quilting cotton, flannel, felt, velvet or faux fur for the cuff. Lining: plain cotton.

Printing

  1. Print at 100% / actual size and check the test square on page A1.
  2. Tape the pages together or print the A0 file at a copy shop.

You will need

  • Outer fabric: 30 × 60 cm (S), 50 × 90 cm (M), 60 × 110 cm (L)
  • Same amount of lining, cuff fabric 45 × 20 cm
  • Optional: fusible fleece for a sturdier stocking

Sewing steps

  1. Cut 2 outer and 2 lining pieces, each pair mirrored (fold the fabric right sides together and cut through both layers).
  2. Sew the two outer pieces right sides together, leaving the top open. Clip the curves and turn right side out.
  3. Sew the lining the same way, leaving a 8 cm gap in the back seam. Do not turn.
  4. Fold the loop strip lengthwise, sew, turn and fold it in half. Pin it to the back seam at the top.
  5. Sew the cuff into a ring (short ends together), fold it in half lengthwise wrong sides together and pin it around the top of the outer stocking, raw edges even.
  6. Slip the outer stocking inside the lining, right sides together, and sew around the top through all layers.
  7. Pull everything through the gap, close the gap, push the lining in and fold the cuff down.

Make it yours

  • Add a name on the cuff with felt letters or embroidery.
  • Use faux fur or a pom-pom trim on the cuff.
  • Make a set of three sizes for the whole family.
