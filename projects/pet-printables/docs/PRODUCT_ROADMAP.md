# Product Roadmap

The product family is fixed and pinned to the **day of the week** (each scheduled
run is a fresh, memory-less session, so day-pinning prevents duplicate work).

## Weekly schedule

### Monday (Hétfő)
1. **Cat Memorial Keepsake Journal** — a gentle, warm (beige-brown variant)
   keepsake for remembering a cat.
2. **New Kitten Starter Kit** — profile, vaccination log, feeding & weight,
   litter training, vet contacts, shopping list, first-week to-dos.

### Tuesday (Kedd)
3. **Pet Health & Vaccination Record Book** — KDP-friendly **logbook** (needs its
   own KDP trim + repeatable interior pages, not just a US Letter one-off).
4. **Pet Sitter Instructions & Info Sheet** — feeding, walks, medication,
   emergency contacts, habits/quirks.

### Wednesday (Szerda) — ✅ both built
5. **Dog Walking & Feeding Log** — habit-tracker style. _Built, verified._
6. **Senior Dog Care Journal** — symptoms, meds, mobility, vet visits, memories.
   _Built, verified (warm variant)._

### Thursday & Friday
No new products. Either:
- a new **colour variant** of an existing product (blush / dusty blue /
  terracotta / greige / charcoal), or
- **Etsy titles + keywords** for earlier products.
If there is nothing to do, say so in one sentence and stop.

## Page plans

Every product = cover + functional pages + "The Small Print." Each page is a
`<div class="page">`. Footer: product name left, page name right.

### ⑤ Dog Walking & Feeding Log (8 pages) — BUILT
1. Cover
2. Read this first (how to use + "this log belongs to")
3. Weekly Walk & Feeding Overview (7-day tick grid)
4. Daily Walk Log (times, length, route, potty, mood + checklist)
5. Daily Feeding Log (meals, portions, water, treats, supplements)
6. Monthly Habit Tracker (walks, training, grooming, dental, play, weigh-in)
7. Notes & Key Contacts (vet, emergency, walker, sitter)
8. The Small Print

### ⑥ Senior Dog Care Journal (8 pages) — BUILT (warm variant)
1. Cover
2. Read this first (what's inside + gentle note)
3. Dog Profile (identity, photo box, care team, conditions/baseline)
4. Daily Wellness & Mobility (7-day tracker + signs to watch + comfort)
5. Medication & Supplement Schedule (schedule table + weekly dose grid + refills)
6. Vet Visits & Questions (visit details, questions, what the vet said, next steps)
7. Memories & Milestones (favourites, milestones, "a note to my dog")
8. The Small Print

### Suggested page plans for the unbuilt products
- **① Cat Memorial Keepsake Journal:** cover · about this journal · "my cat"
  profile + photo · favourite memories · "the day we met / our life together" ·
  a letter to my cat · a place for pawprint / keepsake · the small print. Use the
  **warm** palette throughout.
- **② New Kitten Starter Kit:** cover · welcome/checklist · kitten profile ·
  vaccination & deworming log · feeding & weight chart · litter training tracker ·
  vet & microchip contacts · shopping list · first-week to-dos · the small print.
- **③ Pet Health & Vaccination Record Book (KDP):** cover · how to use · pet
  profile · vaccination record (repeatable) · vet visit log (repeatable) ·
  medication log · weight chart · emergency contacts · the small print. Design for
  a **KDP trim** and many repeated interior pages.
- **④ Pet Sitter Instructions & Info Sheet:** cover · the essentials at a glance ·
  feeding schedule · walk/potty routine · medication · house & habits/quirks ·
  emergency contacts & vet · daily check-in log · the small print.
