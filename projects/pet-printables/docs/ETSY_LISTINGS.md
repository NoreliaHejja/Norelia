# Etsy Listings

Finished listing copy, plus the naming conventions. Market: US + DACH (English
copy). The owner pastes these into Etsy herself.

## Conventions
- **Title:** ≤ 140 characters. Front-load the product + strongest keywords, then
  secondary keywords, then "US Letter PDF Instant Download."
- **Tags:** exactly 13, each **≤ 20 characters**, lowercase, multi-word phrases
  preferred (Etsy weights phrases). No duplicates of the exact title words where a
  variation is more useful.
- **Description (write per listing):** what's inside (page list), who it's for,
  "US Letter (8.5×11 in) PDF," "No physical item will be shipped," AI-assisted
  design disclosure, personal-use licence.
- Avoid trademarks.
