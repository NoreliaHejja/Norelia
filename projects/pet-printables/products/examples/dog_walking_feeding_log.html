Welcome! This log keeps your dog’s daily routine in one tidy place. Print as many copies as you like — one week per sheet, or a fresh daily page when you want more detail. Here’s how to get the most out of it.

How to use these pages

01Weekly overview — tick off each walk, meal, and task across the week at a glance.
02Daily walk & feeding logs — use on busy or extra-detailed days (new puppy, vet notes, portion changes).
03Habit tracker — build gentle routines like training, grooming, and dental care.
04Notes & contacts — keep the vet, walker, and sitter details handy.

A few gentle tips

•  Keep the sheet somewhere everyone sees it — the fridge or a clipboard by the leash.
•  Note portion sizes so every family member feeds the same amount.
•  Jot a word about mood or energy — patterns help you and your vet.
•  Celebrate small wins. Consistency is kindness.

The Small Print

A few friendly notes about using this printable.

Personal use

This file is for your own personal, non-commercial use. Please print as many copies as you need for your household. You’re welcome to enjoy it — but please don’t resell, share, or redistribute the file itself.

A caring companion, not medical advice

This log is a simple organizing tool to help you track routines. It is not veterinary advice. For any concerns about your dog’s health, diet, exercise, or behaviour, always consult your veterinarian. Adult supervision is recommended for all pet care and feeding.

About the design

Artwork and layout were created with the help of AI design tools and carefully reviewed by a human. Colours may vary slightly between screens and printers.

Thank you

Thank you for welcoming this little log into your home. Wishing you and your pup many happy walks and well-earned treats.
