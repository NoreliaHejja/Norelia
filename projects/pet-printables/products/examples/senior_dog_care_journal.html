A gentle note

Watching a dog grow old is a privilege and a heartache all at once. Small, steady observations — a little stiffness here, a smaller appetite there — are some of the kindest gifts you can bring to your vet. Trust what you notice. You know your dog best.

The Small Print

A few friendly notes about using this journal.

Personal use

This file is for your own personal, non-commercial use. Print as many copies as you need for your household. Please don’t resell, share, or redistribute the file itself.

A caring companion, not medical advice

This journal is a tool to help you observe and organize your senior dog’s care. It is not veterinary advice and does not replace professional care. Always consult your veterinarian about symptoms, medications, diet, and any changes you notice. Adult supervision is recommended for all pet care.

About the design

Artwork and layout were created with the help of AI design tools and carefully reviewed by a human. Colours may vary slightly between screens and printers.

Thank you

Thank you for giving your old friend such gentle, attentive love. These quiet days are a gift — this little journal is honoured to hold them.
